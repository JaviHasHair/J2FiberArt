About the studio

Hi, I'm Jennifer McDow — I knit every piece by hand.

What started as a way to use up leftover yarn turned into J2 Fiber Art. Every hat and scarf here is knit on my own needles, one row at a time, using wool and alpaca-blend yarn I choose for warmth first.

I keep batches small on purpose — usually just two or three of each colorway — so every order still feels personal. If something's out of stock, it's because someone else is already wearing it.

  • Hand wash cold, lay flat to dry
  • Merino wool & alpaca-blend yarns
  • Made to order — please allow 1–2 weeks
